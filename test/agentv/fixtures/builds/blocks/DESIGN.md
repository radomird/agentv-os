# Blocks

A falling-block game: pieces of four squares fall into a well ten wide and twenty deep. A full row clears, and the pieces fall faster as rows clear.

- Left and right move the piece, up (or a gamepad's X) turns it, down drops it a row, ok drops it to the floor.
- Back pauses a game in play; Back on the pause leaves. Home pauses it too.
- The best score is kept in the app's storage and shown on its tile.
- Big squares on a dark well, readable from the couch.

Left out: a hold piece, and two players.
