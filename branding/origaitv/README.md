# origaiTV logo

origaiTV is getting a new name: origaiTV, at [origai.tv](https://origai.tv). Origami plus AI: a TV you fold into the shape your home needs. This folder holds the new logo. Nothing uses it yet, and the old origaiTV logo files next door stay in place until the rename lands.

There's one version for each look, in dark and light. "tv" takes the accent colour, and the rest uses the text colour.

- `origaitv-logo-dark.svg`, `origaitv-logo-light.svg`: Terminal, in the same pixel font as the origaiTV logo.
- `origaitv-glass-dark.svg`, `origaitv-glass-light.svg`: Glass, in Manrope, with the mark on a rounded tile.
- `origaitv-cinema-dark.svg`, `origaitv-cinema-light.svg`: Cinema, in Instrument Sans.
- `origaitv-mark-dark.svg`, `origaitv-mark-light.svg`: just the folded play button, on a clear background, for icons.

The colours are Tokyo Night's: `#1a1b26`, `#c0caf5` and `#7aa2f7` on dark, and `#343b58` and `#2e7de9` on light. The Glass and Cinema files have their letters turned into shapes, so they look the same without the fonts installed.
